Privacy

Effective 2026-10-28

Actor

  • Actor has no analytics, tracking or advertising. Your terminals, files and projects stay on your Mac.
  • Actor checks for updates by downloading a release list from GitHub at most every two hours. GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Voice input and AI prompts go only to the API endpoints you configure, under those providers' terms.

Stillmake Cloud

Signing in is optional. It syncs a set of Actor settings between your Macs. When you sign in, we keep:

  • Your Sign in with Apple identifier and the email Apple shares, which may be a private relay address.
  • Each signed-in Mac's computer name and when it signed in and was last used, shown under Settings → Cloud → Sessions.
  • Synced settings: appearance, terminal, editor and Markdown fonts and layout, file tree and panel options, and the voice input and note prompts you write. API keys, endpoints and folders are never synced.
  • Apple's authorization token, used only to revoke the authorization when you delete your account.

Settings travel over TLS and are stored without additional encryption, so do not put secrets in synced prompts. The data is used only to provide sync; it is never sold or shared.

Where and how long

  • The server runs on Alibaba Cloud in Beijing, China. Using Stillmake Cloud transfers this data there.
  • Daily backups of accounts and settings are kept in Microsoft OneDrive for 30 days.
  • A Mac's session ends after 60 days without use, when you sign out, or when you revoke it.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Cloud → Delete Account removes your account, sessions and synced settings immediately and revokes the Sign in with Apple authorization. Backups age out within 30 days.
  • Stopping Sign in with Apple for Actor in your Apple Account signs out every Mac; deleting your Apple Account deletes the Stillmake Cloud account.
  • For access, correction or deletion by request, write to feedback@stillmake.com.
